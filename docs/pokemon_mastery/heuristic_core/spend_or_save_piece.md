# Heuristic: Spend Or Save Piece

Status: live tiny card.

Use when: a low support piece, Explosion user, sleeper, spinner, setter, or
sack candidate may be preserved or spent.

Rule: name the remaining job before spending or saving.

Ask:
- Is the job still live: hazards, Spin, phaze, sleep absorb, pivot, check?
- Is the job complete or now irrelevant?
- Cheapest-cover handoff gate: when Explosion, sleep, phaze, Baton Pass, or a
  boosted attacker is the branch, list the pieces that cover it by remaining
  job cost. The top handoff is the cheapest piece that covers the branch, not
  the strongest owner by type.
- Status-nullifier owner gate: when the named branch is Toxic, sleep, Rapid
  Spin, Spikes, or a RestTalk sleep loop, separately name the piece that makes
  that branch fail outright: status immunity, sleep absorber, spinner,
  spinblocker, phazer, or stable RestTalk owner. Do not substitute generic
  active chip, a resist, or a higher-payoff attacker unless it converts before
  the status/hazard branch acts or preserves the same nullifier for the next
  board.
- Already-statused absorber gate: when the opponent's revealed or strong-prior
  route is sleep, Toxic, paralysis, or other status, include the already
  statused or sleeping piece that can absorb the status while preserving fresh
  route owners. Promote it only if it survives the revealed damage well enough
  or its remaining job is lower value than the fresh phazer, cleaner, support,
  or converter it protects.
- Support cash-out absorber gate: when the support/status user also has
  revealed or strong-prior Explosion, Self-Destruct, Destiny Bond, or another
  cash-out, compare the status move against the cash-out before spending the
  fresh owner. A low, already-statused, sleeping, or completed-job piece can
  outrank a finite-job status/phaze move if the cash-out removes that fresh
  owner before its job matters.
- Support-lane legality gate: before a support move can block cash-out, verify
  the public state says it is legal and nonredundant. Spikes is not a live lane
  if that side already has Spikes; Toxic/status is not live into an already
  statused or immune target; Spin is not live with no hazards to remove.
- What converter opens if this piece attacks, sacks, explodes, or hands off?
- Does a reversible move or handoff cover the same active target and named
  branch while keeping this irreversible converter available for the next
  forced choice?
- Before Explosion or Self-Destruct, what board exists afterward: target
  removed or chipped, target action denied, free-entry owner, exact range
  created, future job lost, and preserving line available?
- If hazards kill this piece on entry, does the controlled sack cancel their
  action and create clean entry for the named owner?
- Against a low revealed sleeper or self-KO candidate, which lower-value
  Normal resist, Ghost, or sack preserves the route piece?
- If revealed Pursuit/trap damage punishes escape, what HP and job remains?
- If a low defensive converter attacks, does it KO or change the next forced
  choice before the return hit removes its job?
- Before preserving a pressured piece, does its current attack, phaze, or
  setup force Rest, deny a reset, or win the next branch this turn?
- If the piece is a RestTalker, spinblocker, spinner, or phazer in a Spikes
  loop, does extra chip actually change the next forced choice before its job
  is spent?
- If setup spends this piece's HP/tempo, does it survive revealed or
  strong-prior coverage that targets its role?
- Does exact removal actually happen before self-KO after priority, speed,
  paralysis, and move order are priced?
- Before raw Explosion or Self-Destruct from a threatened piece, does it move
  first or survive the revealed/strong-prior hit? If not, boom is a read.
- Cash-out survival threshold gate with lower-cost veto: if the self-KO user
  has delivered its job or is a low-cost absorber, and it survives the public
  or strong-prior hit long enough to remove or cripple a central route owner,
  compare lower-cost exact removal and lower-cost owner handoff first. Promote
  cash-out only when ordinary damage, recoil-free coverage, status, or the
  lower-cost owner does not remove, force, or wall the same route in time.
  Demote cash-out when a Ghost/resist branch, faster removal, unclear
  post-trade owner, exact non-self-KO removal, or cheaper owner makes the trade
  unnecessary.
- In a mapped endgame or explosion-stack route, does preserving the lowest-
  future-job exploder serve a named job, or only generic insurance?
- Post-trade remaining-job inventory gate: after any faint, Explosion,
  Self-Destruct, Rapid Spin completion, or sleep/status tax, relabel every
  remaining own piece before ranking: cleaner, blanket owner, status absorber,
  spinblocker, phazer, completed-job support, or sack. The best handoff is the
  lowest-future-job piece that covers the branch. Do not spend or expose the
  cleaner or blanket owner when a completed-job support piece, sleeping piece,
  or lower-future-job absorber covers the same branch.
- Compressed-endgame three-slot gate: after a cash-out, two or more fainted
  pieces, or a forced low-support entry, the top three must include these legal
  roles before ranking: active immediate converter/finish-job, controlled
  sack or stay-in if the active has the lowest future job, lowest-future-job
  bench sack when it creates the clean owner entry, and the defensive owner for
  the opponent's highest-incentive next attacker or pivot. Do not let a narrow
  finite job hide the next defensive owner, and do not preserve a low active or
  low bench piece when its death creates the clean owner entry.
- Handoff triad gate: when switching, preserving, or spending is plausible,
  classify every listed own piece that can enter or stay as direct absorber,
  route converter/status owner, or cheapest scout/sack. The top three must
  include the best legal member of each live class unless the class is
  impossible or strictly dominated. Do not promote the direct absorber until it
  has been compared with the route converter and the cheapest scout/sack.
- Support-owner lane: when the direct absorber is a passive wall and another
  support piece survives the revealed or strong-prior hit well enough to spend
  Toxic, Spikes, Spin, phaze, Protect, sleep/status absorption, or cash-out, the
  support piece is the route converter/status owner lane. Include it in top
  three before the passive wall unless it cannot move before being removed or
  the wall's entry creates a stronger immediate route.
- Top-three slot contract: before ranking, fill these live slots if legal:
  active first-action converter/cash-out that moves before being removed,
  support or offensive route owner that changes the next turn, and stable
  absorber/preservation owner. Do not let wrongness cost delete the active
  converter from top three; demote it after inclusion if the preservation route
  is better. Do not let a passive wall delete the support/offensive owner.
- Target-specific handoff scoring: Baton Pass, switch, phaze drag, and sack
  entries are not generic candidates when targets differ. Score each legal
  target by immediate conversion, entry cost, boost usefulness, status/hazard
  exposure, and the next owner it forces. A handoff target with a unique
  immediate route job must appear by name in top three before a generic
  preserve/switch line can be promoted.
- Doomed-target sink lane: in target-specific handoff scoring, include a low,
  statused, poisoned, or dead-on-reentry target when it can absorb Screech,
  Toxic, Thunder Wave, phaze, scout damage, or another non-damaging route while
  preserving the true owner. This target is not a long-term absorber; it is a
  route-preserving sink. Promote it when the main owner would lose a decisive
  boost, Defense, status state, or HP by taking that branch.
- Handoff target matrix: when any switch, Baton Pass, sack, or preserve/spend
  handoff is plausible, write each live target lane before choosing top three:
  active move, support converter, low/statused or sleeping sink,
  wake-eligible/off-field route owner, and stable support sink. Each lane must
  name the target and its first forced action. Choose the ranked top three from
  this matrix; a target named only in prose but absent from top three is a
  candidate miss when it has a unique first forced action.
- Lane-tagged top three: on handoff turns, each ranked candidate must include
  its matrix lane in brackets, such as `Switch Victreebel [support converter]`,
  `Switch Snorlax [low sleeping sink]`, or `Hidden Power [active converter]`.
  If a live unique lane is omitted, say why it is dominated before reveal.
- Must-cover lane gate: do not choose top three first and add lane tags after.
  In `Critical resource`, write `Must-cover lanes:` and use those lanes as the
  ranked candidate pool. If more than three lanes are live, write
  `Excluded live lane:` with the target and concrete dominance reason. A
  sleeping/off-field route owner, low/statused sink, or stable support sink
  named in the ledger must become rankable unless this exclusion is written.
- Setup lane in must-cover gate: when Curse, Growth, Belly Drum, or repeated
  boosting competes with Rest, attack, phaze, or switch, active setup is a
  separate lane if the user survives to move and the boost changes damage,
  Rest, parity, or phaze timing. Do not hide setup inside active damage or
  preservation.
- Clean-entry sack promotion: the cheapest scout/sack outranks the direct
  absorber when it covers the opponent's highest-incentive attack, phaze, or
  setup turn and creates a clean entry for the converter that would otherwise
  take Spikes plus the hit or be Roared before acting. Keep the direct absorber
  first only when it converts before that branch acts or the sack does not
  create a better entry board.
- Entry-cost veto for route owners: before promoting a switch to the strongest
  converter or matchup owner, price the likely hit, status, phaze, trap, and
  hazard cost it takes while entering. If a lower-future-job active or bench
  piece can spend itself to create the same owner's free entry, rank that
  spend/stay line first unless the owner converts before the entry-cost branch
  acts or the post-sack board gives the opponent a stronger route.
- Pivot-reset candidate pass: after the opponent switches, phazes, or reveals
  the real attacking branch, rebuild top three from the newly active target
  before carrying forward the old route. Include the best current active move
  into that target, the direct absorber, and the lowest-job absorber/sack that
  handles the new target's highest-incentive action. Keep the previous route
  owner top only if it still beats the new target or denies its reset/pass/setup
  branch now.
- Protect-cycle exact-range ledger: in low-piece endgames with Protect,
  Leftovers, poison, sleep turns, or repeated damage, write the last observed
  damage, current HP after recovery, and post-Protect HP before ranking. Accurate
  damage outranks higher-power miss risk when both create the same two-turn
  route. Status or setup outranks damage when damage is not exact and the
  target's next action KOs, resets, or Protect-cycles out of range.
- If a needed converter attacks through miss/crit exposure, does controlled
  sack -> stable revenge remove the same threat while preserving the converter?
- If a boost can be passed, does the current boosted Pokemon die to a revealed
  or strong-prior immediate roll before the damage route converts?
- If a support piece is dying anyway, can it finish its job first with Spikes,
  status, phaze, Spin, or boom before the hit lands?
- Finish-job versus preserve-owner gate: if a finite-job piece is likely to be
  removed before it gets another clean turn, promote the move that completes
  its job now: Spin, Spikes, status, phaze, Rest, or cash-out. Preserve the
  piece only when the handoff keeps the same job realistically available. If
  the tempting active move is merely a possible lure or low-certainty branch
  punish, preserve the revealed-pressure owner instead of spending it.
- Will residual damage end the active target before this slower support piece
  can spend its move?

Preserve if the job still owns a future route. Spend if the target, absorber,
post-trade owner, and lost job are all named.
For Growth/Baton Pass, the boost owner is a route piece. Pass when the receiver
survives the public or strong-prior roll and keeps the boost live; attack only
when the attack converts before the roll can remove the passer.

Cash-out is not a reward for naming Explosion. It becomes top only after the
support job is delivered, irrelevant, or impossible to deliver in time.
Support is irrelevant only after a legality check: the move must still apply to
the current side or target. Do not list redundant Spikes, impossible Toxic, or
empty Rapid Spin as a real support lane.
Explosion can convert without a KO when chip plus the free entry creates a
named exact-range owner; still name the lost job, immunity/resist branches, and
post-trade owner before spending.
If ordinary damage, setup, status, phaze, or a handoff covers both the active
target and the named branch while preserving Explosion, Self-Destruct, Baton
Pass, or another irreversible converter, rank the reversible line first. Spend
now only when delay lets the target reset, escape, remove the user, or
invalidate the route.
Resisted or non-KO self-KO can still convert when chip plus the GSC action skip
plus free entry creates a named owner or exact range that the preserving line
cannot create before reset, phaze, escape, or removal. If the post-boom board is
not better than the best preserving line, keep the converter.
In mapped endgames, do not preserve an Explosion user as vague insurance. If
the named blocker and post-trade owner are known, trade down when it simplifies
the remaining board.
The defensive mirror is mandatory: when their low Snorlax/Cloyster/Exeggutor
can cash out after delivering sleep, hazards, or status, preserve the valuable
RestTalker, special wall, or win piece unless its current move converts before
Self-Destruct/Explosion.
If several pieces can absorb the named branch, spend the one with the fewest
remaining jobs: asleep/low support, completed setter, spent status absorber, or
otherwise stranded piece before a healthy route owner, phazer, cleaner, or
unique special wall. Re-check Surf/EQ/coverage branches before choosing a
resist that only covers Explosion.
Already-statused pieces can be real absorbers, not dead weight. Use them to
catch sleep or status when they survive the revealed hit and protect a fresh
route owner. Do not use them when the revealed damage removes their remaining
job before the status branch matters.
Against support pieces with strong-prior cash-out, do not click a finite-job
status or phaze move with the fresh owner until the low/statused sack branch
has been priced. Status first is correct only if it happens before the cash-out
and losing that owner is acceptable or route-winning.
Conversion veto: cheapest-cover is a gate, not autopilot. Promote the handoff
only when the covered branch is route-critical and the active move does not
convert first. If active Curse, KO damage, phaze, status, or branch-punish
creates the stronger next board before the branch matters, keep active progress
above the cheap handoff and list the handoff as the fallback.
Exact removal beats fear. If the active move KOs the low blocker before it can
phaze, reset, or trade, do not switch to a guard just because self-KO is legal.
But exact removal is not exact if status or speed lets the self-KO move first.
Likewise, your own self-KO cash-out is not live if a revealed faster attack
removes the user before it can move; preserve or hand off unless the miss/switch
read and fallback are named.
After chip into a low support or self-KO user, re-run preserve / coverage /
cash-out / defensive-sack immediately; last turn's correct Surf or attack may
have been the bluff before the trade.

Do not protect a piece with no entry path or cash out into an unpriced immunity.
Do not click obvious coverage from a low route piece if Recover, handoff, or
preservation is what keeps the check alive.
Do not spend a low RestTalker, spinblocker, spinner, or phazer on chip after
poison, Spikes, or Rest pressure has already created the route. Preserve with
Rest, Protect, or handoff unless the attack changes the next forced choice.
Do not preserve by reflex when the current piece can convert before the
handoff and still leaves a named owner afterward.
Do not call Pursuit/trap escape preservation unless the named job survives.
Do not spend a needed converter into miss/crit exposure when a lower-value sack
plus stable revenge produces the same removal and keeps the converter for the
next route.
Do not overguard a support piece whose key job is still unfinished if it can
complete that job before dying. After the job is complete, re-run the cash-out
guard normally.
Do not preserve a finite-job piece by reflex when the current turn is its last
reliable chance to complete that job. Do not make the opposite mistake by
spending a route owner on a possible lure when a revealed attack or support
move already shows the preservation owner is needed.
In compressed endgames, do not rank only active moves. Include the controlled
sack/stay option, the lowest-future-job bench sack when it creates clean entry,
and the next defensive owner before choosing between chip, status, setup, or a
handoff.
Do not let the resource ledger become inert prose. If a switch/handoff is live,
the listed pieces must be sorted into direct absorber, route converter/status
owner, and cheapest scout/sack before ranking. Omitting a legal class from top
three is a candidate miss; ranking a direct absorber first requires a named
reason it beats both the route converter and the cheapest scout/sack this turn.
Do not collapse route converter/status owner into the hardest wall. If a
support piece lives the likely hit and can immediately Toxic, set Spikes, Spin,
phaze, Protect-cycle, absorb status, or cash out, list that support owner as a
separate top-three lane before choosing the passive wall.
Do not let preservation erase a faster active route. If the active move can
move first and plausibly KO, force Rest, phaze, deny reset, cash out, or create
a decisive range before the active is removed, it belongs in top three even
when the miss/wrongness cost is high.
Do not write "Baton Pass" or "switch" as if the target is implied. If Snorlax,
Rhydon, a phazer, a status absorber, or a low sack creates a different next
board, name the target in the ranked candidate and price that target's first
forced action.
Do not exclude a low poisoned or dead-on-reentry piece from handoff targets
just because it is not a stable owner. If it can absorb Screech, Toxic, Thunder
Wave, phaze, or a scout turn that would cripple the real owner, it is a legal
sink candidate and must be named.
Do not let the `Critical resource` field list pieces without making them
rankable. On any handoff turn, convert the prose ledger into a target matrix:
target -> first forced action -> route gained or protected. Build top three
from that matrix.
On active Explosion, Self-Destruct, Destiny Bond, or low-piece
irreversible-status turns, use a compact candidate census before top-three
compression: active reversible/setup/support, active irreversible, direct
absorber, route owner, and branch-immune or low-job pivot. Choose top three
from that census. If a live job is omitted, write the concrete dominance reason
in `Critical resource` before reveal.
Do not write untagged top-three switch/pass candidates on handoff turns. The
lane tag is the check that the matrix actually constrained ranking.
Do not choose top three and then decorate it with lane tags. Write
`Must-cover lanes:` inside `Critical resource`, then copy those lanes into the
ranked list. If a live lane is excluded, name it as `Excluded live lane:` and
state the dominance reason before reveal.
Do not omit active setup from a must-cover list when Curse, Growth, Belly Drum,
or repeated boosts can change the next damage, Rest, parity, or phaze
threshold. Rank it below Rest or damage if needed, but make it visible.
Do not send the direct converter through Spikes plus the main hit when a low
sack would create the same converter entry cleanly. Promote the sack first
unless the converter acts before the hit, denies phaze/setup immediately, or the
post-sack board gives the opponent a stronger route.
Do not carry yesterday's route into a new active target. After a switch, phaze,
or newly revealed attack, re-run active move / direct absorber / cheapest
absorber before ranking; omitting any of those live classes is a candidate miss.
Do not call a move exact without carrying forward the last damage and recovery.
In Protect/Leftovers endgames, price the blocked turn first; if Protect turns a
KO into non-exact damage, status or accurate two-turn damage may outrank the
flashier attack or setup.
Do not count a slow support spend if poison or other residual will remove the
target before the support move executes.
Do not set up with a route piece into strong-prior coverage that removes it
unless the line is explicitly a high-risk read with a named payoff.
