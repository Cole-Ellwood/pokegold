# Boss AI code review, 2026-09-08

Scope: every file in `engine/battle/ai/` (40 files, 36,463 lines) at `898e0ee5`,
reviewed by eleven read-only Opus lanes against the no-cheating rule, the
SM83 register rules (`farcall` clobbers, cross-bank calls), stale invariants
and one-sided fixtures, with every finding re-verified by the lead before it
was fixed or listed. Bugs rank above simplifications. Line numbers are those
of `898e0ee5` unless a row says otherwise.

## What a player would notice

Four bugs in the shipped game ROM changed what bosses do, and all four are
fixed in this session's commits. A boss deciding whether to switch was
comparing its confidence against garbage instead of its tier threshold
whenever it was considering its win-condition Pokémon or was inside a switch
cooldown, so on those turns it switched almost every time or never; that is
gone. The "what role is the player's last move" read that feeds the same
switch decision returned nonsense for every move (Roar looked like a status
move plus a trap plus a wall-breaker), so switch confidence got up to 18
free points on the wrong moves; it now returns the right role. A boss whose
ace was Mean Looked or Wrapped on its first turn lost its once-per-battle
Haki for good; it now still gets the Haki move read and only the switch half
is refused. Super-effective coverage moves (an Electric move from a
non-Electric boss into a Water type) never consulted the KO-band oracle that
sharpens KO pressure, so bosses undervalued their best coverage; they consult
it now. Two more bugs affected bookkeeping rather than decisions: after the
player's Ditto or Transform, the boss forgot to mark that Pokémon fainted and
could file its moves under the copied species; fixed. One bug lived only in
the offline selector that has no gameplay caller yet (identity replies
against a bench mon that faints to Spikes); fixed. No hidden-information read
was found anywhere in the 40 files: every lane enumerated every player-side
read and found only public state.
